# Turn the Globe - 2 Minute Demo Script

## Goal

Show the core idea fast: a user can start from a global view, choose a country, select a defining historical moment, and step into an immersive panorama built from verified facts and generated media.

## Script

### 0:00 - 0:15

"Turn the Globe is an interactive historical atlas. The idea is simple: every country has a turning point, and we let you start from the whole Earth and then step directly into that moment."

### 0:15 - 0:35

"On the homepage, the globe is the entry point. You can rotate it, click any country, and the interface focuses that region while surfacing its timeline on the right. For the polished demo, Spain is our fully built path."

Action:
- Rotate the globe a little.
- Click Spain.

### 0:35 - 0:55

"Once Spain is selected, the URL reflects that state, and the right side shows the key events for that era. This lets the experience feel spatial first, but still gives you a precise timeline to move through."

Action:
- Briefly point at the event list.
- Click one event year.

### 0:55 - 1:25

"Clicking an event opens the panorama view with the country and event passed into the route. Here the user moves from the abstract globe into an immersive scene tied to a specific historical moment."

"This panoramic view is rendered in 3D, so instead of watching a flat clip, you can look around inside the environment."

Action:
- Let the panorama load.
- Drag the view left and right.

### 1:25 - 1:45

"What matters behind the scenes is that the live app makes zero AI calls at runtime. The content is generated ahead of time, reviewed, committed into the repo, and then served instantly during the demo."

"The pipeline is: Cala provides verified historical facts, OpenAI turns those facts into narrative and generation prompts, and Fal AI produces the visual assets."

### 1:45 - 2:00

"That gives us something we care about a lot: a storytelling experience that feels cinematic, but stays grounded in a verified data layer. You begin with the whole planet, choose a country, choose a moment, and then step inside history."

Action:
- Pause on the panorama for a final hero moment.
- Optionally use `Back` to return to the prior state, or `Home` to return to the globe.

## One-Line Close

"We built a globe where you do not just read about history - you travel into the exact moment that shaped a place."
